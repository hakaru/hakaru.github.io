TineModeler

Physical Modeling Synthesizer — Fender Rhodes Reimagined

Welcome to TineModeler support page.

TineModeler is a physical modeling synthesizer that recreates the Fender Rhodes electric piano sound from first principles — no samples, just physics.

Features

Physical Modeling

Every aspect of the Rhodes sound is generated in real-time using mathematical models: tine vibration (Euler-Bernoulli beam), hammer impact (Hertz contact force), electromagnetic pickup (Faraday's law), and felt damper simulation.

AUv3 Plugin

Use TineModeler as an Audio Unit plugin in GarageBand, Logic Pro, Cubasis, and other AUv3-compatible hosts.

Real-Time Parameters (Pro)

Adjust pickup position, magnetic field strength, hammer stiffness, and more — shaping the sound in ways that sample-based instruments cannot.

Lightweight

Runs efficiently on Apple Silicon. No large sample libraries to download.
