## FAQ

### Q: What devices are supported?
TineModeler requires iOS 16+ (iPhone/iPad) or macOS 14+ (Mac).

### Q: How do I use TineModeler with GarageBand?
Open GarageBand, create a new track, tap "Audio Unit Extensions", and select TineModeler.

### Q: Can I use an external MIDI keyboard?
Yes. TineModeler supports CoreMIDI — connect via Bluetooth MIDI, USB MIDI, or Virtual MIDI.

### Q: How do I restore my Pro purchase?
Open TineModeler, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases".
