1TakePro

Pro Recording App — Audio, Camera & Multi-Device Sync

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Introduction

1TakePro ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

Summary

1TakePro collects anonymous diagnostic data to improve app stability and user experience. All your audio and video recordings remain stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to any server. Multi-device sync transmits only clock timing signals — no audio or video data leaves your device.

Recordings

All audio and video recordings you create with 1TakePro are:

  • Stored locally on your device only
  • Never uploaded to any server
  • Accessible only to you
  • Exportable via the Files app or Share Sheet

Data We Collect

1TakePro uses Firebase, a service provided by Google, to collect anonymous diagnostic data. This helps us improve app stability and understand how the app is used.

Crash Data (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app crashes, we automatically collect:

  • Device type and iOS version
  • App version and build number
  • Crash logs and stack traces
  • Memory and disk usage at time of crash

Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We collect anonymous usage data including:

  • App opens and session duration
  • Feature usage patterns
  • Device type and iOS version
  • Anonymous device identifier (not linked to your identity)

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your audio or video recordings, or any content you create
  • Personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Data for advertising purposes

Tracking and Advertising

We do not track you for advertising purposes. 1TakePro:

  • Does not use advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Does not share data with advertisers
  • Does not track you across other apps or websites
  • Does not require App Tracking Transparency permission

Microphone and Camera Access

1TakePro requires microphone access for audio recording and camera access for video recording. These permissions are:

  • Used solely for recording functionality within the app
  • Only active when you explicitly start a recording
  • Controllable via iOS Settings at any time
  • Never used to capture data in the background

Multi-Device Sync (Local Network)

1TakePro's multi-device sync feature uses your local Wi-Fi network to synchronize recording clocks across devices. This works as follows:

  • Only clock timing signals are exchanged between devices — no audio, video, or personal data
  • All sync communication stays on your local network and is never sent to external servers
  • Local Network permission (iOS prompt) is required to discover other devices on the same Wi-Fi

Third-Party Services

1TakePro uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Crashlytics - Crash reporting and diagnostics
  • Firebase Analytics - Anonymous usage analytics

These services are provided by Google. For more information, see Firebase Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

Crash and analytics data is retained by Firebase according to their standard retention policies. This data is anonymous and cannot be used to identify you.

Data Deletion

You can delete your recordings at any time through the app's Recordings tab. Uninstalling the app will remove all local data from your device.

Children's Privacy

1TakePro does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The anonymous diagnostic data we collect is not linked to any individual user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
