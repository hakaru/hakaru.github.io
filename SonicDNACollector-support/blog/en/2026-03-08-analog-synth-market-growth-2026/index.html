Analog Synth Market Continues Rapid Expansion

The analog synthesizer market is projected to grow from $1.2 billion in 2024 to $2.5 billion by 2033, representing a CAGR of 9.2%. In 2025-2026 alone, the global market is expanding from $1.34 billion to $1.40 billion.

What's Driving the Growth

  • Project Studio Proliferation: Over 70% of professional-quality music is now produced in project studio environments
  • Eurorack Modular Explosion: The modular format continues to create new market opportunities
  • Analog Beat Synth Demand: Growing from $101.3M in 2026 to $140M by 2034 (CAGR 5.7%)
  • Active Used Market: Vintage and current gear sees robust trading activity
