We're excited to announce the launch of SonicDNA Collector!

What is SonicDNA Collector?

SonicDNA Collector is an iOS app designed for audio engineers, plugin developers, and gear enthusiasts who want to capture the sonic characteristics of analog audio equipment.

Whether you're modeling a vintage compressor, analyzing a tube preamp, or documenting the behavior of your favorite gear, SonicDNA Collector provides the tools you need.

Key Features

Precision Sweep Generation

Generate logarithmic sweeps from 20Hz to 20kHz at 96kHz sample rate for accurate frequency response measurements.

Gain Staircase Recording

Automatically record multiple sweeps at different gain levels to analyze nonlinear behavior and distortion characteristics.

Real-time Monitoring

Watch your levels with dual VU meters and FFT spectrum displays for both input and output.

Debug Console

Track every step of the recording process with the built-in debug console.

Getting Started

  1. Connect your USB audio interface
  2. Route your equipment in the signal chain
  3. Calibrate levels with the 0dBFS test tone
  4. Record and analyze!

Check out our User Manual for detailed instructions.
