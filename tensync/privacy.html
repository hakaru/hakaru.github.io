TenSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-25

TenSync ("the app") collects a limited amount of data to keep the app working well and to support free distribution through advertising. We do not require an account, do not store data on our own servers, and do not share data beyond what is described below.

Data we collect

  • Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics): When the app crashes, an anonymized crash report is uploaded so we can fix bugs. The report contains stack traces, device model, OS version, and a randomly generated install identifier. No personal information is included.
  • Analytics (Firebase Analytics): Anonymized usage events such as app opens and screen views are sent to Google Analytics. This helps us understand which features are used. No precise location, no contact information, no user account is involved.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): TenSync displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use a per-device identifier (IDFA, only with your permission via the iOS App Tracking Transparency dialog) to personalize ads. If you decline tracking, you still see ads but they are not personalized.

Data we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, or contact information.
  • No precise location.
  • No photos, contacts, microphone, or camera.
  • No data uploaded by you to our servers.

Local storage

Your best score and settings are stored on your device only, via iOS UserDefaults. They never leave the device.

Sync mode

When TenSync discovers other TenSync devices on the same Wi-Fi network, the app exchanges only the protocol messages needed to synchronize the start of a round (clock-sync packets and a "start at this time" command). No personal data is shared between peers, and the messages do not leave the local network.

Third-party privacy policies

  • Google (Firebase + AdMob): https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's privacy

TenSync does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The age rating is 4+ but the app is not directed at children under 13.
