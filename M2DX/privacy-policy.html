M2DX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-26

M2DX is a MIDI 2.0 FM synthesizer for iOS and macOS. This policy explains what information M2DX collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

M2DX collects the following anonymous, pseudonymous diagnostic data:

  • Crash Data — stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, and the in-memory state of the app at the moment of a crash.
  • Performance Data — launch times, memory usage, and other anonymous performance metrics.
  • Product Interaction — anonymous usage events (e.g. "preset loaded") to help prioritize improvements.

None of this data is linked to your identity. We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact information
  • Account credentials (M2DX has no user accounts)
  • Audio recordings, MIDI performances, or any musical content
  • Location data, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or device identifiers beyond Apple's standard Identifier for Vendor (IDFV)

How we use this data

The data above is used solely to detect and fix bugs, monitor app stability, and prioritize improvements. We do not sell or share this data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Third-party services

M2DX uses Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config) to collect the data described above. Firebase processes this data on Google's servers under Google's privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Tracking

M2DX does not track users across other apps or websites. We do not show App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompts because we do not engage in cross-app tracking.

Your rights

To request deletion of your diagnostic data, contact hirose@hakaru.net with your device's IDFV. The IDFV is shown at the bottom of the M2DX Settings → About screen.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hirose@hakaru.net.
