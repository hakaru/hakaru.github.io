Sound Tuning That Works While You Play

1Take v1.4 introduces AI Auto-Optimization, a feature that listens as you record and continuously refines your audio settings on the fly. You no longer have to stop a session to tweak EQ curves or dial in compression ratios. The AI engine handles it, and every adjustment is visible right in the recording detail view.

This is not a one-size-fits-all loudness normalizer. It is a parameter-aware optimization loop that reads the character of your sound and selects values specific to that take.

What Gets Optimized

The AI engine targets four processing stages, adjusting each one based on the live audio signal:

EQ (Equalizer)

Spectral analysis identifies problem frequency bands - muddiness in the low-mids, harshness in the upper-mids, excessive room resonances - and sets corrective filter values. Both frequency and gain are adjusted per band so cuts and boosts land exactly where the signal needs them.

Compression

Dynamic range is measured continuously throughout the recording. The optimizer sets threshold, attack, and release values appropriate to the source material - gentle and transparent for vocals that already sit in a natural range, tighter and faster for instruments with sharp transients.

Input Trim

Noise floor detection and LUFS measurement combine to determine whether the recording is sitting too hot or too quiet. The trim value is adjusted to place the signal in the optimal range for downstream processing, preventing clipping and ensuring headroom for mixing.

Saturation

When a recording sounds thin or sterile, a small amount of harmonic saturation can add presence and warmth. The AI evaluates spectral density and harmonic content before applying saturation, keeping the result musical rather than distorted.

Inline Transparency: See Every Change

Adjustments do not happen silently. Every parameter the AI modifies is displayed inline in the recording detail view, showing the previous value and the new value side by side. If you disagree with a change, you can override it manually. The display covers all four stages so you always know exactly what was touched and why.

This level of transparency is intentional. AI-assisted processing should inform your workflow, not obscure it.

Learning Across Takes: Cross-Preset Propagation

AI Auto-Optimization does more than optimize each recording in isolation. It learns from the results of previous takes and carries those insights forward. When a particular EQ approach consistently improves recordings under a given preset, those tendencies inform the starting point for the next session.

This cross-preset propagation means the optimizer gets more accurate as you use 1Take more. Early takes benefit from the general analysis engine; later takes benefit from both the engine and the accumulated knowledge of your specific recording environment and style.

DAW Integration via markers.json

Optimization history travels with your recordings. When you export a take, the markers.json file includes a complete record of every parameter change the AI made, along with the analyzed file name and both the previous and new values for each adjustment.

In your DAW, this data serves as a reference for the processing already applied to the audio. You can match your DAW chain to the 1Take settings, work from the documented starting point, or use the history to understand what was happening in the room during that session.

Field Description
analyzedFile Name of the recording file that was analyzed
parameter The processing parameter that was adjusted
previousValue The value before optimization
newValue The value after optimization

Technical Details

The analysis pipeline runs entirely on-device during recording. There are three core measurement systems:

  • Spectral analysis - Continuous FFT-based frequency analysis identifies the energy distribution across the audio spectrum, informing EQ decisions in real time
  • Noise floor detection - Measures the ambient noise level during quiet passages to establish a baseline for gain and trim decisions
  • LUFS measurement - Integrated loudness is tracked against broadcast and streaming reference levels, providing the foundation for compression and trim adjustments

All processing runs locally on your device. No audio data is sent to any server.

How to Enable AI Auto-Optimization

  1. Open Settings in 1Take
  2. Tap Recording
  3. Enable the AI Auto-Optimize toggle

Once enabled, the optimizer activates automatically for every new recording. Existing recordings are not retroactively modified. To apply optimization to a previous take, open its detail view and use the manual optimization option.
