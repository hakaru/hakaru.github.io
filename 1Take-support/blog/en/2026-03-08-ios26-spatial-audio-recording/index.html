iOS 26 Transforms iPhone Audio Recording

At WWDC 2025, Apple announced sweeping improvements to audio recording in iOS 26. For creators and recording app developers alike, this is the most significant audio update in years.

Spatial Audio Recording Goes Open

iOS 26 opens Spatial Audio recording to third-party apps via AVAssetWriter. Using the iPhone's built-in microphone array, apps can now capture 3D sound scenes in Ambisonics format.

Real-Time Audio Processing

CaptureSession now supports simultaneous MovieFileOutput and AudioDataOutput. This means apps can record while simultaneously processing and visualizing audio in real time.

Audio Mix API

A new API lets apps balance foreground and background audio sources with granular mixing control.

AirPods as Pro-Quality Microphones

iOS 26 adds a new media tuning mode to AirPods — specifically tuned for music and content creation recording instead of call-optimized processing.
