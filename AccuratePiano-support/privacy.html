ACCURATE PIANO

Privacy Policy

A sampled grand piano for iPhone and iPad.

Effective date: October 19, 2026 Operator: hakaru (Hakaru Hirose)

Accurate Piano is an app for playing a sampled piano on iPhone and iPad.

Information handled by the app

The app processes MIDI input and onscreen keyboard input on your device to produce sound. Settings such as level, touch response and effects are stored on your device. The app does not send this performance data or these settings to the operator's servers.

No account is required. The app does not include advertising, analytics SDKs that transmit usage data, or tracking across other apps and websites.

Storage and deletion

Local app settings are removed when you delete the app. Device backups managed by iOS / iPadOS follow your operating system settings. The app has no separate cloud account or synchronization service.

Support requests

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to attach. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate problems, not for advertising or tracking. We retain it only as long as needed for support and delete information when it is no longer needed. To request deletion of your support correspondence, contact us below.

External links and Apple services

Links to instrument sources, licenses and other external resources open third-party websites. Those websites and Apple's App Store and operating system services handle information under their own privacy policies.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

This support website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process IP addresses and request information to deliver and protect the website. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.
